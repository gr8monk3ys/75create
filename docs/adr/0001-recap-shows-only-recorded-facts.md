# The recap and certificate show only recorded facts

The MVP spec (F7) listed "total minutes est." among the recap stats. The app
never measures time: a day is made by its rules, a log and an artifact, not
by a timer. An estimate would be a number the app invented and then printed
on a certificate as if it were earned, so the recap and certificate show only
what was recorded — days made, longest streak, logs written, pieces kept.

If time ever matters, it comes from something the person records (a
"minutes" field on the log), not an estimate.
